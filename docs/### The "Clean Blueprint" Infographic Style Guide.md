
### The "Clean Blueprint" Infographic Style Guide

**Background & Canvas**

* **Color:** Clean, solid, very light blue/grey.
* **Elements:** Zero gridlines, zero measurement marks, and absolutely no background clutter.
* **Overall Aesthetic:** Clean, professional whitepaper illustration style.

**Typography & Text**

* **Font Choice:** Large, clear, sans-serif font to ensure absolute legibility.
* **Color:** Dark blue text for main elements and headers; white text when placed inside dark header bars.

**Design Motif: Nodes & Cards**

* **Structure:** All distinct subjects, inputs, or outputs must be styled as individual "cards."
* **Card Header:** A dark blue header bar at the top with white text.
* **Card Body:** A solid white body below the header.
* **Card Illustration:** Inside the white body, include a clean, blueprint-style line drawing rendered in blue and black ink.

**Connections & Flow**

* **Arrows:** Thick orange arrows with distinct arrowheads.
* **Routing:** Arrows should clearly converge or diverge to show the flow of data or processes between sections.

**Containers & Grouping (If needed)**

* **Main Container:** Large rectangular containers must have a thick orange border and a dark blue text header.
* **Internal Divisions:** If the container holds multiple sections, stack them in horizontal boxes separated by thin orange lines.

---

### How to use this in a prompt:

When you are ready to make your next infographic, start your prompt like this:

> "Create a highly legible, professional technical infographic diagram. **[Insert your layout instructions here, e.g., A top-to-bottom flowchart explaining the video rendering process]**.
> **Apply the following exact style:** The background is a clean, solid, very light blue/grey with zero gridlines, zero measurement marks, and no background clutter. All text must be rendered perfectly in a large, clear, sans-serif font. All nodes must be styled as distinct "cards" featuring a dark blue header bar with white text, and a white body below containing a clean, blueprint-style line drawing in blue and black ink. Connect the cards using thick orange arrows..."
