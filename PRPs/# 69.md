

# Product Requirements Prompt: Fix "Double Line" & Handle Alignment

**Severity:** BLOCKER
**Diagnosis:** The user sees two boundaries because the visual "White Line" is centered in the handle (offset by 15px), while the "Region Edge" is at the actual cut point.
**Goal:**

1. **Kill the Dark Strip:** Force the handle container to be 100% transparent.
2. **Align to Edge:** Move the White Line (Pole) and Flag to the **absolute edge** of the handle, removing the gap.

## 1. The Corrected CSS Logic

**File:** `src/components/tools/AmbiTrim.tsx`
**Action:** Replace the `<style>` block with this geometry-corrected version.

