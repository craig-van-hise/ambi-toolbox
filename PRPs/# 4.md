**1. Context & Constraints**

* **Goal:** Convert between the two industry-standard Ambisonics formats:
1. **AmbiX** (ACN Order, SN3D Normalization) — *The Modern Standard.*
2. **FuMa** (Furse-Malham Order, MaxN Normalization) — *The Legacy Standard.*


* **Excluded:** Do NOT implement TBE or Facebook 360 formats.
* **Hard Constraint (FuMa Limit):** FuMa is mathematically defined **only up to 3rd Order** (16 channels).
* *Logic:* If user attempts to convert a >3rd Order file to FuMa, the app must **Fail with an Error** (e.g., "FuMa format does not support orders higher than 3rd").

**3. Backend Requirements (`electron/handlers/ambiswap.ts`)**

**A. The Channel Mapping (Re-ordering)**
You must construct a `pan` filter in FFmpeg to remap the channels.

* **1st Order (4ch):**
* AmbiX (W, Y, Z, X)  FuMa (W, X, Y, Z)
* *Map:* 0$\to\leftrightarrow\leftrightarrow\leftrightarrow$1.


* **Higher Orders:**
* Use the standard ACN vs. FuMa sorting tables.
* *Reference:*
* FuMa Sequence: W, X, Y, Z, R, S, T, U, V, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q
* ACN Sequence: W, Y, Z, X, V, T, R, S, U, Q, O, M, K, L, N, P





**B. The Normalization Math (Critical!)**
You cannot just swap channels. You must apply Gain because **SN3D** (AmbiX) and **MaxN** (FuMa) use different scaling.

* **Rule 1: The W Channel (Omni)**
* **AmbiX  FuMa:** Gain  (approx `0.7071`).
* **FuMa  AmbiX:** Gain  (approx `1.4142`).


* **Rule 2: The Directional Channels**
* For **1st Order**, the directional channels (X, Y, Z) usually have a 1:1 gain relationship between SN3D and MaxN.
* *Implementation Note:* Construct the `pan` filter using these coefficients.
* *Example (FuMa  AmbiX, 1st Order):*
`pan=4|c0=1.4142*c0|c1=c2|c2=c3|c3=c1`
*(Notice: c0 gets gain, and channels 1,2,3 are swapped).*
