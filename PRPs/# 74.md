## 2. Action 1: Restore the 26-Point 3D Grid

You must revert to the 26-point Lebedev grid. To pass 26 custom channels from the `pan` filter to the `sofalizer` filter, you must use FFmpeg's custom channel layout syntax: **`26c`**.

* **Pan Syntax:** `pan=26c|c0=...|c1=...|...|c25=...`
* **Sofalizer Syntax:** `sofalizer=sofa='...':speakers='c0 az el|c1 az el|...|c25 az el'`

*Note: Azimuth and elevation in the `speakers` string must be in degrees. Ensure no trailing pipes (`|`) exist in the string.*

## 3. Action 2: Correct the AmbiX (ACN/SN3D) Math

The Ambisonic input is 3rd-Order AmbiX. This means it uses **ACN channel ordering** and **SN3D normalization**. If your matrix calculations are incorrect, the audio will phase-cancel and lose volume.

Your JavaScript matrix generator must calculate the Spherical Harmonic weights (Y_n^m) strictly using SN3D formulas.
For a given speaker at azimuth \theta (radians) and elevation \phi (radians):

* Channel 0 (W): 1.0
* Channel 1 (Y): \sin(\theta) \cos(\phi)
* Channel 2 (Z): \sin(\phi)
* Channel 3 (X): \cos(\theta) \cos(\phi)
*(Extrapolate the standard SN3D formulas for orders 2 and 3).*

The final pan weight for Channel `c` to Speaker `s` is:
W_{c, s} = Y_n^m(\theta_s, \phi_s) \times \text{node_weight}_s \times \text{MaxRE_Factor}_n
