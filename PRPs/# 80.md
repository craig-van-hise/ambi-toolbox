**Context:** The 3rd Order Lebedev Grid is currently misaligned with FFmpeg's `hexadecagonal` layout, causing a 180-degree sector fold in the upper-rear hemisphere. Top-Back-Center (TBC) and Top-Back-Right (TBR) are swapped.

* Index 13: TFC (Top-Front-Center)
* Indices 14 & 15: Must remain empty/padding (TFL, TFR).



### Task 2: Gain Staging Architecture (The Volume Fix)

**Target File:** `main.ts`
**Context:** The current linear volume compensation (`scaleFactor * 2.5`) fails to account for the  -15dB insertion loss introduced by Head-Related Transfer Function (HRTF) convolution, resulting in critically low output volume.

3. Append the correct volume filter after the binaural node: `[binaural];[binaural]volume=${totalGain}[out]`
* **Constraint:** Ensure the matrix normalization step (L1 Norm scaling where ) is mathematically decoupled from this makeup gain.
